            Calculation methodology and implemented equations
            

              

                Derived wave statistics
                σ = (Hs,max − Hs,mean) / 5.6
He = Hs,mean + 5.6σ
Hm = Hs,mean − 0.3σ
                
The C++ fallback logic is retained when the estimated σ is zero.

              

              

                Birkemeier (1985)
                hc = 1.75 He − 57.9 [He² / (g Te²)]
                
Field-adjusted inner limit used for the active nourishment profile toe.

              

              

                Hallermeier (1981)
                hc = 2.28 He − 68.5 [He² / (g Te²)]
                
Analytical inner limit used for hard-structure and scour considerations.

              

              

                Houston (1995)
                hc = 6.75 Hs,mean
                
Simplified preliminary parameterisation based on annual mean wave height.

              

              

                Hallermeier (1983)
                hc = 0.018 Hm Te √[g / ((s − 1) D50)]
                
Outer sediment-entrainment limit, with D50 converted from millimetres to metres.

              

              

                Sensitivity scenarios
                H ±25%; T ±25%; combined H+25% and T+25%
                
Base, low/high energy, short/long period and worst-case scenarios.
